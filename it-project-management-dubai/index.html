IT project management and on-site technical leadership in Dubai

Senior on-site technical leadership for IT migrations, deployments, office moves and cutover weekends in Dubai. Also available to MSPs and consultancies under their brand.

Quick answer·Reviewed 27 October 2026

Viewstate supplies a senior technical lead for IT projects in Dubai that need experienced judgement in the room: migrations, deployments, office moves and cutover weekends.

The role is hands-on. It means directing your engineers or a partner's team, owning the runbook and being the person who decides when a step is safe to proceed.

Engagements are scoped per project, from a single cutover weekend to a multi-month programme, and can be delivered white-label for MSPs and consultancies.

01 / Scope

What this covers

On-site technical lead
A senior engineer-operator on site for migrations and deployments, directing the team executing the work rather than watching from a call.
Office moves and rollouts
Planning and running the technical side of a move: connectivity, cabling coordination, desk moves, device deployment and the first morning after.
Cutover weekends
Runbooks, rollback plans and a named decision-maker for the window when the business cannot afford to be down on Monday.
Vendor and partner coordination
Holding ISPs, hardware suppliers and software vendors to their commitments so the project timeline is not hostage to them.
White-label delivery for MSPs
Senior project support delivered under a partner's brand when a managed service provider needs extra capability for a specific engagement.
02 / Fit

Who this is for

  • A company with an IT project that keeps slipping because nobody owns the technical decisions.
  • An internal team with the skills but not the seniority to call a cutover.
  • An MSP or consultancy that has sold a project it needs senior delivery capacity for.
  • A business opening or relocating an office in the UAE on a fixed date.
03 / How it works

How an engagement runs

  1. Day 0

    Discovery call

    Thirty minutes, no cost. The situation, the timescale and whether Viewstate is the right fit are settled before anything is proposed.

  2. Week 1–2

    Assessment

    A current-state review of the relevant systems, vendors, risks and costs, ending in written findings and a prioritised list.

  3. Week 3+

    Delivery

    Hands-on ownership of the agreed work, with a weekly status update and the decisions recorded as they are made.

  4. Close

    Handover

    Documentation, runbooks and a clean transfer to your team or provider, so the outcome does not depend on one person staying.

04 / Engagement

Ways to work together

  • Embedded. Senior contract support inside your team, consultancy or MSP, on site or remote, for a defined period.
  • Project. A clear scope, agreed deliverables and experienced ownership of the work, quoted as a fixed scope.
  • Advisory. Ongoing senior judgement for priorities, vendors and recurring decisions, on a light monthly cadence.

Pricing follows scope: a project is quoted after the discovery call, and ongoing work runs as a monthly arrangement rather than a retainer for unspecified hours.

05 / Questions

Common questions

Do you manage the project or do the technical work?
The engagement is technical leadership, which usually means both. The deliverable is a project that lands: runbooks, cutover plans, decisions taken at the right moment, and a documented handover at the end.
Can you work with our existing IT team or MSP?
Yes. This is the normal shape of the work: Viewstate leads the technical plan and directs the people executing it, whether they are internal engineers or a partner's team.
What is a cutover weekend engagement?
A defined window for a migration or deployment where downtime has to be contained, typically Friday night to Sunday, with a tested rollback path and a go/no-go decision point before the change starts.
Do you work under an MSP's brand?
Yes. Project support for managed service providers is delivered white-label under the partner's brand, as an extension of their delivery capability.
