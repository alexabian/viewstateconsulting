Software asset management and technology cost review in Dubai

Software Asset Management and technology cost review in Dubai: Microsoft 365, Adobe and Zoom licence reviews, renewal and procurement strategy, compliance and audit readiness.

Quick answer·Reviewed 27 October 2026

Viewstate connects what a company actually uses to what it pays for, then fixes the gap in both directions: unused licences cancelled, real needs covered, renewals negotiated from evidence.

The work covers Software Asset Management, licence reviews across Microsoft 365, Adobe, Zoom and similar, renewal and procurement strategy, and audit readiness.

Engagements are a single review project, or an ongoing light cadence so renewals never arrive unplanned again.

01 / Scope

What this covers

Software Asset Management
A working inventory of what is installed, assigned and actually used, kept current instead of reconstructed during an audit.
Licence reviews
Microsoft 365, Adobe, Zoom and comparable estates: which tier fits which population, where duplication exists, and what can be reduced at renewal.
Renewals and procurement
Renewal calendar, negotiation preparation and vendor comparison, so decisions are made on evidence before the deadline rather than after it.
Compliance and audit readiness
Positioning for a vendor audit with documentation that answers the question before it becomes an invoice.
Vendor cost review
The wider technology spend: telecoms, cloud, support contracts and hardware refresh, reviewed as one picture rather than separate line items.
02 / Fit

Who this is for

  • A company whose software spend has grown without anyone owning the decision.
  • A business facing a vendor audit or a renewal it does not understand.
  • An organisation that has been through layoffs or restructuring and is still paying for the old headcount.
  • A managing director who suspects over-licensing but has no evidence either way.

05 / Questions

Common questions

What is Software Asset Management in practice?
An accurate, maintained record of what software is licensed, to whom, at what tier, and whether it is used, tied to contracts and renewal dates. It exists so decisions are made on evidence rather than assumptions.
Will a licence review actually reduce cost?
Often, yes: unused assignments, wrong tiers and duplicated tools are common. Where a reduction is not available, the outcome is still useful, because you know what you are paying for and what it covers.
Can you help before a Microsoft or Adobe audit?
Yes. Audit readiness is part of the work: reconciling entitlements against deployment and usage, closing the gaps that are cheap to close now, and documenting the position.
Do you sell software licences?
No. Viewstate is independent and does not resell licences, so the recommendation is not tied to a commission on what you buy.
