Interim and part-time IT manager in Dubai

Interim and part-time IT management in Dubai for companies without an IT manager or between hires. Vendor oversight, IT set-up for new UAE offices, ITIL-aligned service processes.

Quick answer·Reviewed 27 October 2026

Viewstate provides interim and part-time IT management in Dubai for companies that do not have an IT manager, or are between hires.

The includes are practical: owning vendors and MSPs, keeping projects moving, setting up IT for a new UAE office, and putting service processes in place that people follow.

Engagements run part-time, typically on a monthly basis, and end with a documented handover rather than a dependency.

01 / Scope

What this covers

Part-time or interim IT manager
A named senior IT manager for a defined number of days per month, with the authority to make decisions and the discipline to document them.
Vendor and MSP oversight
Choosing, briefing and holding IT suppliers accountable, and stopping the pattern where each renewal is negotiated by whoever happens to answer the email.
IT set-up for new UAE offices
Offices of companies based in Japan, Europe and elsewhere: connectivity, devices, Microsoft 365, local suppliers and the bridge back to headquarters IT.
Service desk processes
Service desk and support workflows aligned with ITIL, sized to the company rather than copied from an enterprise manual.
IT priorities and budget
A rolling view of what matters, what it costs and what to stop paying for, in language a managing director can act on.
02 / Fit

Who this is for

  • A company with no IT manager and no intention of hiring one full-time.
  • A business whose IT manager has left and needs cover while it recruits.
  • A foreign company opening its first UAE office that needs local senior IT ownership.
  • A managing director who cannot tell whether the IT spend is reasonable.
03 / How it works

How an engagement runs

  1. Day 0

    Discovery call

    Thirty minutes, no cost. The situation, the timescale and whether Viewstate is the right fit are settled before anything is proposed.

  2. Week 1–2

    Assessment

    A current-state review of the relevant systems, vendors, risks and costs, ending in written findings and a prioritised list.

  3. Week 3+

    Delivery

    Hands-on ownership of the agreed work, with a weekly status update and the decisions recorded as they are made.

  4. Close

    Handover

    Documentation, runbooks and a clean transfer to your team or provider, so the outcome does not depend on one person staying.

04 / Engagement

Ways to work together

  • Embedded. Senior contract support inside your team, consultancy or MSP, on site or remote, for a defined period.
  • Project. A clear scope, agreed deliverables and experienced ownership of the work, quoted as a fixed scope.
  • Advisory. Ongoing senior judgement for priorities, vendors and recurring decisions, on a light monthly cadence.

Pricing follows scope: a project is quoted after the discovery call, and ongoing work runs as a monthly arrangement rather than a retainer for unspecified hours.

05 / Questions

Common questions

How does interim IT management differ from hiring an IT manager?
It is a commitment of days per month rather than a permanent headcount, and it is built to end. The objective is to stabilise, document and either hand over to a hire or continue on a light retainer.
How many days per month is typical?
Most engagements start at two to four days per month and adjust to what the situation needs. The scope is agreed before the engagement starts and reviewed as it goes.
Can you manage a new office set-up before we have staff on the ground?
Yes. IT set-up for new UAE offices of overseas companies is a recurring engagement: connectivity, hardware, Microsoft 365, local suppliers and coordination with headquarters IT.
Do you replace our MSP?
Usually not. Viewstate typically sits above the MSP, owning priorities, vendor management and budget, while the MSP keeps delivering day-to-day support.
