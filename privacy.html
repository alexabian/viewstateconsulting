Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

This page explains what personal data viewstate.co collects, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it to the minimum needed to run the site and reply to enquiries.

Who we are

Viewstate Consulting FZCO is an independent IT consultancy based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For anything about your data, email contact@viewstate.co.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. If you email us, book a call through Microsoft Bookings or send a brief through Microsoft Forms, we receive what you choose to share: usually your name, company, email address and message. We use it only to reply and to discuss the work.
  • Website analytics. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how the site is used: pages viewed, approximate location (country and city), device and browser type, and which contact links are clicked. It does not tell us who you are. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and Google receives only basic, cookieless signals.

We do not use advertising cookies, sell personal data or share it for marketing.

Who processes the data

  • Microsoft (Microsoft 365: email, Bookings and Forms)
  • Google (Google Analytics)
  • GitHub and Cloudflare (hosting and network delivery of this site, which involves standard server logs)

These providers may store data outside the UAE under their own security and privacy commitments.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails and form responses are kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting work, then deleted. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by emailing contact@viewstate.co. We'll reply within 30 days.
