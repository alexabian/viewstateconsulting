IT infrastructure and operations consulting in Dubai

Hands-on IT infrastructure consulting in Dubai: VMware, Hyper-V and Proxmox virtualisation, Veeam backup and restore testing, UniFi networks, and security health checks.

Quick answer·Reviewed 27 October 2026

Viewstate provides hands-on infrastructure ownership for Dubai companies that depend on systems nobody is actively looking after.

The work centres on four things: virtualisation, backup that has actually been restore-tested, network and Wi-Fi, and the security basics that quietly fail over time.

Engagements are a defined project or an ongoing monthly commitment. Delivery is remote-first, with on-site work in Dubai and the GCC for physical changes.

01 / Scope

What this covers

Virtualisation
VMware, Hyper-V and Proxmox environments reviewed, consolidated and cleaned up, including host capacity and guest sprawl.
Backup and recovery
Veeam backup designed against what the business actually needs to survive, and restore-tested rather than assumed to work.
Networks and Wi-Fi
UniFi networks, switches, wireless and cameras, plus secure remote access for staff who work outside the office.
Security health checks
A structured review of endpoints, patching, accounts and remote access, with a prioritised remediation list.
Operational ownership
Documentation, runbooks and vendor coordination so the environment stops depending on one person's memory.
02 / Fit

Who this is for

  • A company whose infrastructure grew organically and is now hard to change or explain.
  • An organisation that has never actually tested a restore from its backups.
  • A business moving offices, adding a site or relocating a server room.
  • A team that needs senior technical capacity for a defined period rather than a full-time hire.

04 / Engagement

Ways to work together

  • Embedded. Senior contract support inside your team, consultancy or MSP, on site or remote, for a defined period.
  • Project. A clear scope, agreed deliverables and experienced ownership of the work, quoted as a fixed scope.

05 / Questions

Common questions

What does an infrastructure assessment cover?
Hosts and virtual machines, storage and capacity headroom, backup configuration and job history, network design and wireless coverage, accounts and remote access, patching state and the asset inventory. The output is a written findings document with a prioritised action list, not a sales document.
Do you test backups or just configure them?
Both. Backup configuration without a tested restore is only an assumption. Restore tests are part of the delivery, and the result is recorded so you know what recovery actually looks like.
Can you support an environment another provider manages?
Yes, in a defined scope. It is also common to work alongside an existing MSP, owning infrastructure and network layers while they handle service desk.
Do you cover both on-premise and cloud infrastructure?
Yes. On-premise virtualisation and networks, Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, and hybrid arrangements where both sides have to agree with each other.
