Microsoft 365 consulting in Dubai

Independent Microsoft 365 consulting in Dubai: tenant set-up, email migration, Entra ID and conditional access, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and ongoing remote administration.

Quick answer·Reviewed 27 October 2026

Viewstate is an independent IT consultancy in Dubai that sets up, secures and administers Microsoft 365 and Entra ID for small and mid-sized companies.

Typical work: a tenant build or a migration off an on-premise Exchange server, tightening sign-in and email security, then running the tenant on a monthly basis.

Engagements are fixed-scope projects or a monthly remote service. Delivery is remote, with on-site days in Dubai and the GCC when the work calls for it.

01 / Scope

What this covers

Tenant set-up and email migration
Greenfield tenants and moves from on-premise Exchange, including Exchange hybrid coexistence while both systems run in parallel.
Entra ID, Active Directory, groups and conditional access
Joining devices, structuring groups and roles, and putting sign-in rules in place that people can live with.
Email security and deliverability
SPF, DKIM and DMARC configured and tested, so mail reaches inboxes and the domain cannot be spoofed easily.
Ongoing administration
Day-to-day Microsoft 365 administration, remote, as a monthly service rather than a permanent hire.
Licence and tenant hygiene
Reviewing what is actually assigned and used, and cleaning up the sprawl that accumulates over time.
02 / Fit

Who this is for

  • A company moving off an ageing on-premise mail server, or off a tenant nobody documented.
  • An office that has grown past ad-hoc IT and needs identity and email security done properly.
  • A business with no IT manager that wants a named senior contact for Microsoft 365 questions.
  • A new UAE office of a foreign company that needs a tenant set up the way its headquarters expects.

05 / Questions

Common questions

How long does a Microsoft 365 migration take?
A small tenant with mail and file data typically runs two to four weeks from kick-off to handover: assessment, staged migration, cutover weekend, then a stabilisation period. Exchange hybrid or multi-site environments take longer, and the estimate is set after the discovery call.
Can you take over a Microsoft 365 tenant another provider set up?
Yes. This is common. The starting point is a documented review of the tenant, what is licensed, who has access and how sign-in and mail security are configured, followed by a prioritised list of what needs fixing.
Do you work remotely or on site in Dubai?
Microsoft 365 administration is delivered remotely, which keeps it efficient. On-site time in Dubai is available where a migration, cutover or training session is better done in person.
Do you replace our IT support company?
Not necessarily. Viewstate often works alongside an existing support provider, owning the tenant, identity and security layer while day-to-day helpdesk stays where it is.
